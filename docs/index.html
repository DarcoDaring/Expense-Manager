Expense Manager

A personal Android app for tracking daily expenses, income, accounts, credit cards, subscriptions and EMIs.

  • Track spending by category and payment method, including split payments.
  • Credit card bills, EMIs and subscription reminders.
  • Reports and analysis of where your money goes.
  • Optional PIN and biometric app lock.
  • Optional Google sign-in to back up and sync your data to your own Google Drive.
