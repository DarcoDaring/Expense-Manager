Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Expense Manager ("the app") is a personal finance app. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data you enter

Expenses, income, accounts, cards, subscriptions, EMIs, notes and settings that you enter are stored in a database on your device. The app has no servers of its own, and the developer cannot see your data.

Google sign-in (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and profile photo to show your profile, and asks for access to the hidden "App data" folder of your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). The app stores a backup copy of your data in that folder so you can restore it on a new phone and keep devices in sync. Only this app, signed in as you, can read that folder. Signing in is optional; the app works fully without it.

How data is used

Your data is used only to run the features of the app, including backup and sync. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any models. The app contains no analytics or advertising tools.

App lock

If you enable app lock, your PIN is kept in your device's secure storage. Biometric checks are handled by your device; the app never receives your fingerprint or face data.

Deleting your data

You can sign out from the More tab at any time. To remove the cloud copy, delete the app's data from your Google account (Google Account settings, Data and privacy, Third-party apps and services). Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.
